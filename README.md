# CrackInterviewAI: AI Interview Assistant for Windows (Real-Time Answers from Voice, Text and Screenshots)

**CrackInterviewAI** (also searched as "Crack Interview AI") is an AI interview assistant for Windows 10 and Windows 11. It captures an interview question from the interviewer's voice, a typed prompt or a screenshot, and shows a short, structured answer you can say in your own words. It uses your resume, target role, tech stack and job description as context, and it understands English, Hindi and Hinglish speech.

New accounts get **30 free minutes**. After that you buy prepaid time from **₹99 for 30 minutes** in India or **$3.99 for 1 hour** internationally. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

## What CrackInterviewAI does

1. **Captures the question.** From the interviewer's voice through your computer's audio, from text you type or paste, or from a screenshot of a coding problem or multiple-choice question.
2. **Adds your context.** Your resume, target role, tech stack and the job description, if you choose to add them.
3. **Shows a short answer outline.** Written to be spoken aloud: the main point first, then the supporting steps, code or example.

| Input | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Voice | Transcribes the interviewer's question from system audio in English, Hindi or Hinglish |
| Text | Type or paste a question and get a structured answer |
| Screenshot | Reads coding problems and multiple-choice questions from the screen |

## Features

- **Real-time answers** for live interview questions, with the answer starting as soon as the question is captured in AUTO mode
- **MANUAL mode** that waits for your shortcut key, for when you want to decide which question to answer
- **Resume-based answers**: project and experience questions are answered from your own resume
- **Role and job-description context** so answers match the position you applied for
- **Screenshot mode** for coding problems and MCQs
- **Hindi and Hinglish support**: interviewers in India often mix Hindi and English; the app transcribes both and replies in clear English
- **Phone Mirror**: scan a QR code and read the live question and answer in a phone browser on the same Wi-Fi network, with no phone app to install
- **Keyboard-shortcut workflow**, so you do not need to click around another window during a call
- **Works alongside Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams** calls on the same computer
- **Session reports** to review questions and answers after practice
- **Prepaid time that carries over**: unused minutes in a fixed pack stay in your account

## Interview types covered

- Coding and DSA rounds (arrays, strings, trees, graphs, dynamic programming, complexity)
- System design rounds
- Technical rounds on Java, Python, JavaScript, React, Angular, SQL and other stacks
- AI engineer rounds: generative AI, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, fine-tuning, MLOps
- HR and behavioral rounds (tell me about yourself, strengths and weaknesses, STAR answers)
- Resume and project walkthroughs
- Mock interviews and practice sessions

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit and 32-bit installers are provided)
- Internet connection
- A working audio output device (speakers, wired headset or Bluetooth headset)
- There is no macOS, Linux, Android or iOS app in this repository. Phone Mirror shows answers in a phone browser but needs the Windows app running.

## Install in three steps

1. Download `CrackInterviewAI-x64.exe` (most computers) or `CrackInterviewAI-x86.exe` (32-bit Windows) from the [latest release](https://github.com/crackinterviewaiin/App/releases/latest).
2. Run the installer and sign in with your CrackInterviewAI account. You can create one free at https://www.crackinterviewai.in/.
3. Use the 30 free minutes to test voice capture, a typed question and a screenshot on your own headset and meeting app before you buy time.

Full walkthrough: [Windows setup guide](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/windows-setup-guide). If the interviewer's voice is not transcribed, see the [audio troubleshooting guide](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/blog/ai-interview-assistant-not-hearing-audio-fix).

Download the installer only from this repository or the official website.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is CrackInterviewAI?**
CrackInterviewAI is a Windows desktop AI interview assistant. It turns a spoken, typed or screenshot interview question into a short answer outline, using your resume and role as context.

**Is CrackInterviewAI free?**
The download is free and every new account gets 30 free minutes. After that, prepaid packs start at ₹99 for 30 minutes in India or $3.99 for 1 hour internationally.

**How much does CrackInterviewAI cost?**
India: ₹99 for 30 minutes, ₹349 for 3 hours, ₹799 for 10 hours, ₹599 for a 7-day unlimited pass or ₹2,499 for 30 days unlimited. International: $3.99, $8.99, $14.99, $9.99 and $29.99 for the matching plans. These are base prices on 10 November 2026.

**Is there a subscription?**
No. Every plan is prepaid and nothing renews on its own. You pay again only if you choose to buy more time.

**Does unused time expire?**
Time in the fixed packs (Basic, Standard and the hour pack) does not expire. The Weekly Pass and Monthly Unlimited run for 7 and 30 days.

**Does CrackInterviewAI work on Mac?**
No. It runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 only.

**Does it understand Hindi?**
Yes. It transcribes English, Hindi and Hinglish speech and answers in English.

**Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?**
Yes. It runs as a separate Windows app next to your meeting app and listens to the computer's audio. See the guides for [Zoom](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/blog/zoom-ai-interview-assistant), [Google Meet](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/blog/google-meet-ai-interview-assistant) and [Microsoft Teams](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/blog/microsoft-teams-ai-interview-assistant).

**Can I use it for coding interviews?**
Yes. Screenshot mode reads a coding problem or MCQ from the screen and returns an approach, code and complexity. See the [AI coding interview assistant](https://www.crackinterviewai.in/ai-coding-interview-assistant) page.

**Can I see answers on my phone?**
Yes. Phone Mirror shows the live question and answer in a phone browser after a QR scan. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network or the phone's hotspot.

**Can I buy it from outside India?**
Yes. Outside India prices are shown in US dollars and international Visa or Mastercard is accepted. See the [international site](https://www.crackinterviewai.com/).

**Which is the cheapest AI interview assistant in India?**
At published prices with the AI included, CrackInterviewAI costs about ₹116 an hour on the ₹349 pack and about ₹80 an hour on the ₹799 pack. The table above lists other tools with the date each price was checked.

**Is it allowed to use an AI assistant in an interview?**
That depends on the employer and the assessment platform. Use it for preparation, mock interviews and wherever the rules allow assistance, and always answer in your own words.

**Where is the official download?**
Only here, in the [releases of this repository](https://github.com/crackinterviewaiin/App/releases/latest), and on https://www.crackinterviewai.in/download.
